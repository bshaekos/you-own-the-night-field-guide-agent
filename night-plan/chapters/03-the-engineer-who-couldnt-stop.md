## Summary

- James Chen spent six years building the kind of engagement systems this whole book is about, on the inside, with full knowledge of exactly how the twelve-second window gets tuned. Knowing the trick from the inside did not make him immune to it, and knowing changed nothing about his own evenings on its own.
- The chapter makes the case, as plainly as it's made anywhere in the book, for friction over awareness. What changed something for Chen was a small, specific, almost insultingly simple move — the kind this book's Field Guide is built to draw out of a reader directly.
- Chen reappears later in the book, in a very different context, once he's turned the same engineering instinct toward building something that works for him instead of against him.

## The Four Questions

| Four Questions | |
