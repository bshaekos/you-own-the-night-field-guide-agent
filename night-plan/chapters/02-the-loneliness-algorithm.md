## Summary

- Carol Watkins is not lonely by any measure you could put a number on — 847 followers, a group text that throws off forty messages a day, two book clubs. She is also, in a way she had trouble naming, profoundly alone from seven to midnight most nights.
- The feed isn't built to make anyone miserable. It's built to keep a person a few degrees off — not content, because a contented user puts the phone down. The chapter walks through why contact isn't connection, and why Carol's own professional gift (she edits books for a living; she can tell a live sentence from a merely correct one in thirty seconds) had never once been pointed at her own evenings.
- What moved her was an old-fashioned phone call, on a Thursday, the kind that still asks for ninety uninterrupted minutes. What it did to her Tuesdays and Thursdays after that is the chapter's payoff — only that she deleted nothing, and reorganized everything.
