## Summary

- Tom and Ellen Reyes, two years after the conversation that opened the book, are living the follow-up chapter nobody expects to be good news — the one where the new habits usually fade and the couch quietly reabsorbs everyone who briefly escaped it. That isn't what happened to them.
- The forty-five minutes had held, with the ordinary seasonal give and take of real life. What had grown in the space it opened was the surprise — neither of them had set out to produce anything, only to not lose each other, and the producing turned out to be a downstream effect nobody could have planned for.
- What Tom says on that second visit, about what he was actually reclaiming when he reclaimed his evenings, is the sentence the whole closing chapter is built around. It isn't only time. The rest is his to say, in the chapter itself.
