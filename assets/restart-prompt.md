# Restart prompt

Use this when you've fallen off your Night Plan. Not to redo the interview — just to get back on.

```text
I fell off my Night Plan. Use assets/three-move-restart.md and my
existing Rules of Engagement section to walk me through my three restart
moves, in order. Don't ask me to redo any of the other five headings.

If I don't have restart moves written yet, help me write them now, using
the three-move-restart protocol: short, concrete, and runnable on any
night regardless of circumstance.
```

## Why this is a separate prompt

The Field Guide treats interruption as guaranteed, not exceptional — the skill is the restart, not a perfect record. This prompt exists so that one bad night doesn't require rebuilding the whole document. Three moves, in order. Nothing else counts that first night back.
